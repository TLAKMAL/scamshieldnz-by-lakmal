03 / HOW THE DEMO WORKS

Every point has a reason.

Text is converted to lowercase, then checked against eight predefined groups. Each matching group adds its weight once, even when several terms match. The channel is recorded but does not change the score.

Indicator groupExample cuesWeight

Demo thresholds
7+ points: high risk indicators. 4–6: medium risk indicators. 1–3: some warning indicators. 0: no listed indicators detected.

Design weights, not validated probabilities
These initial weights express relative concern. Words such as “pay” or “bank” can appear in legitimate messages. Matching an organisation name is a cue, not verified impersonation.

Adaptation limits
This demo reproduces the documented groups and weights, with explicit word/phrase matching. It is not an export of the original Power Fx formula and may produce different outcomes. It does not separately classify marketing spam or ambiguous content.
